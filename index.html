How Regenerated.com Vets Clinics

Every clinic on the platform is checked against public databases including the OIG exclusion list and state medical boards. Verified clinics undergo manual review of credentials, treatment offerings, patient reviews, and facility standards. The vetting process ensures patients can trust the clinics they find on the directory.

About Regenerated.com

Regenerated.com was built because finding a trustworthy regenerative health clinic shouldn't require weeks of research. The directory aggregates clinic data from across the US, standardizes profiles, and gives patients a single place to compare providers, treatments offered, patient reviews, and credentials.

Whether you're exploring stem cell therapy for a joint injury, HBOT for post-concussion recovery, or peptide therapy for systemic inflammation, Regenerated.com connects you with clinics that have been reviewed against the platform's quality standards.
