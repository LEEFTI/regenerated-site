What Is Regenerative Medicine?

Regenerative medicine is a branch of healthcare focused on repairing, replacing, or regenerating damaged cells, tissues, and organs to restore normal function. Unlike conventional medicine that often manages symptoms, regenerative therapies aim to address root causes by harnessing the body's own healing mechanisms or introducing biological materials that stimulate repair.

The field spans a wide range of treatments, from well-established therapies like platelet-rich plasma (PRP) injections to cutting-edge approaches like exosome therapy and mesenchymal stem cell treatments. As research advances and clinical evidence grows, regenerative medicine is becoming an increasingly important option for patients with chronic conditions, autoimmune disorders, degenerative diseases, and injuries that haven't responded to conventional treatment.

Treatments You Can Find on Regenerated.com

Stem Cell Therapy

Stem cell treatments use the body's master cells to repair damaged tissue. Clinics offer autologous (your own cells) and allogeneic (donor) stem cell therapies for orthopedic injuries, autoimmune conditions, neurological disorders, and anti-aging protocols. Common sources include bone marrow, adipose tissue, and umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells.

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT)

HBOT delivers 100% oxygen at increased atmospheric pressure, driving oxygen deep into tissues to accelerate healing, reduce inflammation, and promote new blood vessel growth. FDA-cleared for 14 conditions including wound healing and decompression sickness, with growing research supporting its use in TBI, Long COVID, and neurological conditions.

Peptide Therapy

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that signal specific biological processes. Therapeutic peptides like BPC-157 (gut and tissue repair), thymosin beta-4 (wound healing), and sermorelin (growth hormone secretion) are used by regenerative clinics to target inflammation, recovery, immune function, and cellular repair.

IV Therapy and NAD+

Intravenous nutrient therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and compounds like NAD+ directly into the bloodstream for maximum absorption. NAD+ infusions in particular have gained attention for their role in cellular energy production, DNA repair, and age-related decline.

PRP and Prolotherapy

Platelet-rich plasma therapy concentrates your blood's growth factors and injects them into injured areas to accelerate healing. Prolotherapy uses dextrose or other solutions to trigger a controlled inflammatory response that stimulates tissue repair. Both are commonly used for joint pain, tendon injuries, and musculoskeletal conditions.
